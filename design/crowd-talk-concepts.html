Janta, talking

Three minimal ways to make the crowd grid feel like a live discussion instead of a scoreboard. Same pitch, same 60 people, same script in each, so you can compare them fairly.

The pitchGhar-ka-khana tiffins for PG students. ₹2,999 a month, Pune and Bengaluru.
1

Talking grid

Keeps today's grid. Whoever speaks lights up and a bubble rises from their square. A thread links them to the person they answered, and the squares around them nod in a ripple, like a room reacting.

Janta · 60 peoplelive
Why it worksSmallest change to what's built. The ripple makes 60 squares feel like 60 people.
Best forBig crowds (500 to 1,000). Only the speaker and their neighbours animate, so it stays fast.
2

Sabha ring

The crowd sits in a circle around your pitch, like a panchayat. The speaker grows, a line runs from them to the centre, and their words fill the middle. The person they're answering gets a dotted line too.

Janta · 60 peoplelive

The crowd is gathering…

Why it worksThe most "share this" frame: a square, centred, readable on a phone, like a story post.
Best forUp to ~150 people. Above that the ring would show a sample and say so.
3

Huddles

Everyone starts undecided at the top. Each speaker says their piece and walks over to their side, and a few quiet listeners follow them. You watch the crowd split into groups as it argues.

Why it worksShows influence: you see opinions pull people over. Strong as a short screen recording.
Best forUp to ~200 people as dots. Larger crowds would move in clusters.
