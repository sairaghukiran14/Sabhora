Before the shoot

checklist

Screen capture

  1. Run each room once for real on the tiffin idea (about ₹2 of AI calls in total). Keep the run IDs.
  2. Record each run with its replay at phone width (about 390px wide, 3× scale) in light mode.
  3. Janta: use the 100-person crowd so the grid fills the frame.
  4. Roast: point it at Sabhora's own landing page, or a demo page you made for this. Never a real company's page.
  5. Swap every figure on this sheet (offers, scores, quotes) for what your recorded run actually said.

Live action

  1. One founder (actor or you), a laptop, a hostel or PG room. Natural light.
  2. Mom and friend are voice only (speakerphone, voice note). No faces needed.
  3. Film reactions as close-ups for picture-in-picture: wince, lean in, smile, face-palm, relief.
  4. Record clean room tone and the pitch line on a lav mic.

Must stay true

  • Say “simulated personas” and “AI investors”. Never “real Indians” or “know if your idea will work”.
  • No Shark Tank name, logo, set look or theme music. The five sharks are originals.
  • CTA is “50 free credits”. Paid plans aren't live yet.
  • Hold publishing until the Sabhora name and trademark checks are done.

Sound and edit

  • Original or licensed track only: tabla and drum build, then a crowd swell, then a comic sting.
  • Captions: two lines max, centred in the lower third, clear of the platform UI.
  • Speed-ramp every loading wait. Hold 1s on each payoff frame.
  • Cover frame: the “I'm out” stamp, or the lit Janta grid.
