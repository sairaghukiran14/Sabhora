## Verdict

3/10, pass. StockSaathi wants to make kirana reorders one tap on WhatsApp, but right now it has 18 paying Indore shops, ₹4,812 a month, and no distributor revenue to prove it.

**Risks**

- Revenue is only about ₹4,812 a month from 18 paying stores against roughly ₹900 field-sales CAC per shop, so the payback period is unproven.
- Distributors pay nothing yet, so the model depends on their goodwill and leaves StockSaathi exposed if a distributor holds stock or withdraws.
- A bigger player like JioMart or Udaan could copy the one-tap WhatsApp reorder flow, and the pitch shows no switching costs or proprietary data.

**Next steps**

- Pull payment history for all 40 Indore shops for the last three months to show how many paid consecutively and what the monthly churn rate is.
- Run the planned distributor fee test with at least five distributors and document whether they pay, what they pay, and whether it changes their stock-holding behaviour.
- Write a one-page unit economics sheet covering CAC, gross margin at 70 percent, and payback period per shop, and test whether field-sales cost can drop below ₹900 per shop.
