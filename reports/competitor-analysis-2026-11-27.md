# Sabhora: competitor analysis (27 November 2026)

## Bottom line

- **We found no product with our exact mix:** investor debate with deal terms, an Indian crowd that votes, and a landing-page roast. This comes from a limited web search, so it does not prove none exists.
- **No single feature is safely unique.** AI investor panels, offers and simulated buyers all exist elsewhere.
- **We are behind on market readiness:** we are not launched, cannot take payments, have no public traffic, and have not shown that our output predicts real behaviour.
- **Position as:** an India-first tool that finds objections and lets founders rehearse. Not as an "idea validator" that predicts success.

## How to read this

- **Traffic** is Similarweb's estimate, from pages headed October 2026. It is the 3-month total. "Monthly average" is that total divided by 3. Estimates for sites this small are very rough.
- **Country and source shares** are desktop only.
- **Competitor features and user counts** are their own claims. We did not sign up for or use any product.
- **"Not stated"** means the page we read did not mention it. The feature may still exist.
- **Our column** comes from our code, docs and test reports.

At ₹0.83 a credit, one Sharks run costs a Pro user about **₹12.45**, Janta-25 about **₹8.30** and Roast about **₹4.15**. Our measured AI cost in recent runs was ₹0.13-0.96. We are cheap per use, but many rivals sell a different thing (a report, or a month of access), so this is not like for like. Cheapness alone will not sell; we may be underpriced.

Funding at the top end (press and aggregator reports, not confirmed by us): Simile $200M Series B at a $2B valuation; Aaru Series A at a headline $1B. They sell to large companies and are not our direct fight today.

## 4. Closest rivals, one by one

| Rival | What they do better | What we do better (against what their pages state) |
|---|---|---|
| **PitchDesk** (India) | Voice or text pitch, "multilingual" AI VCs, 6+ personas, deck creation; payment tiers listed | Our offers have code-enforced valuations and limits, counter and bidding. Their page mentions negotiation rounds but not explicit offers. We add a crowd and a roast |
| **VirtualSpeech** | Established platform, browser and VR, spoken practice, claims negotiation and In/Out | India context, Hinglish voices, 5 investors to their 3, scored verdict with 30-day steps |
| **PitchPrep** | Live mock pitches with real investors, which AI cannot replace | Far lower entry price, five-investor debate, offers |
| **PromptWars shark simulator** (hackathon project) | Also claims follow-ups, walk-outs, negotiable offers and a rewritten pitch. We do not rewrite the pitch | Account, credits, saved runs, crowd and roast. **This shows the Sharks idea is easy to copy** |
| **IdeaProof** | Market size, competitor matrix, claims linked sources, business plan, PDF export, most search traffic | Live debate where the founder answers back |
| **Preuve AI** | Claims 60+ live sources with links, 50 simulated buyers, competitor alerts | Larger crowd option (100), Indian personas, debate and follow-up chat |
| **DimeADozen** | Claims 500+ citations per full report | India: their page says non-English-speaking markets are a poor fit |
| **ValidatorAI** | Free, fast (under 60 seconds), large claimed user base | Much more depth than a score form |
| **mockcrowd** | Personas built from real posts it cites; compares your pitch with a **bland control pitch** | Hosted product, Indian crowd, nothing to install |
| **Synthetic Users / Societies** | Research methods, your own data, security compliance, published accuracy claims | Self-serve and affordable for an early founder |
| **FounderPal and paid roasters** | Marketing toolkit; RoastPage.ai and ConvertRoast sell conversion reports (search listings only) | Six distinct reviewer voices. **But our Roast reads text only, yet gives a Design score** |

## 6. Outside evidence on simulated buyers

Search summaries, full papers not read: one 2025 preprint reports good match with human purchase-intent data using a particular method. Other work warns that AI answers vary less than real ones, can overstate brand choice, and are not yet a replacement for real demand data.

## 7. What to do next, in order

1. **Done on 27 November:** the claim "who would actually pay" was removed from the Janta card, because our tests do not support it.
2. **Finish what blocks launch:** name and trademark checks, payments, reset email.
3. **Fix Roast:** remove the Design score, or give reviewers a screenshot.
4. **Test a control pitch in Janta** and show results relative to it. Real-AI testing costs money.
5. **Add a downloadable report.**
6. **Try PitchDesk, IdeaProof, Preuve and VirtualSpeech by hand** with the same pitch. This is the biggest hole in this analysis.
7. **Compare Sabhora with a plain ChatGPT or Claude prompt** on that pitch. If users see no difference, features will not matter.
8. **Test search as a channel:** one free India-focused tool and a few pages.
9. **Prove one thing:** compare crowd objections with interviews of real people.

Later: voice replies, more Indian languages, sourced market data.
